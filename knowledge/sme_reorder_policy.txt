SME Inventory Reorder Policy

Purpose: Define when store managers must initiate replenishment and how quotations are solicited before purchase.

Reorder Trigger:
- An item requires reorder when current_stock is strictly below reorder_threshold (as recorded in the inventory system).
- Reorder decisions are made in code; the AI assistant compares quotations only after a deterministic reorder flag is raised.

Quotation Solicitation:
- For items below threshold with zero quotations on file, the store manager must solicit quotes from at least two registered suppliers before comparison.
- For purchases in the UGX 500,000–5,000,000 band, procurement policy requires a minimum of three quotations before approval.

Quotation Validity:
- Only quotations with a valid_until date on or after the operating reference date may be used for comparison.
- Expired quotations must be re-solicited; they are flagged as non-binding and inactionable.

Minimum Information on Supplier Quotations:
- Supplier name, unit price in UGX, quantity available, delivery terms (location, lead time, transport responsibility), and quotation validity date.
- Missing delivery terms create cost uncertainty and must be flagged for clarification before order placement.

Human Approval:
- The AI provides objective comparison only. Final supplier selection and purchase order issuance require authorized manager approval.
