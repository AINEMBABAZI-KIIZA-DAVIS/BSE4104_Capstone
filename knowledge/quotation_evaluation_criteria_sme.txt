Standard Quotation Evaluation Criteria for SME Small Purchases

Scope: Applies to internal quotation comparison for replenishment and small purchases (typically under UGX 5,000,000) before requisition drafting.

Required Quotation Fields:
- Unit price in Uganda Shillings (UGX)
- Quantity available relative to replenishment need
- Delivery terms (lead time, location, who pays transport)
- Quotation validity date (valid_until)
- Optional notes (MOQ, split-order constraints, payment terms if non-standard)

Evaluation Dimensions (for human reviewer; AI compares objectively, does not score or select):
1. Total financial outlay: unit price × quantity needed to cover deficit (including MOQ surplus if applicable)
2. Fulfillment coverage: whether available quantity meets or exceeds the replenishment deficit
3. Delivery speed and logistics: lead time and transport terms affecting total landed cost
4. Validity and actionability: expired quotes are inactionable until refreshed
5. Policy compliance: number of quotes meets procurement_policy_ug.txt thresholds for the purchase value band

Risk Flags the Reviewer Should Note:
- [EXPIRED - INACTIONABLE]: validity date before operating reference date
- [MISSING TERMS - CLARIFICATION REQUIRED]: delivery or payment terms not specified
- [SUPPLY SHORTFALL]: available quantity less than deficit (split order required)
- [PRICING ANOMALY]: unit price more than 2.5× the median of competing quotes
- [SINGLE QUOTATION - NO COMPETITION]: only one quote on file

Cross-Reference: Weighting for formal vendor scoring (Price 40%, Quality 30%, Experience 15%, Delivery 15%) is documented in vendor_evaluation_criteria.txt. Quotation comparison for reorder does not apply those weights automatically; it surfaces tradeoffs for human decision.
