Supplier Code of Conduct Addendum – SME Local Purchasing

This addendum supplements the base Supplier Code of Conduct for registered suppliers serving ProcurePrep SME clients in Uganda.

Local Registration:
- Suppliers must hold a valid trading license registered in Uganda.
- Preference is given to locally registered suppliers per procurement policy.

Quotation Integrity:
- Quoted prices must be firm for the validity period stated on the quotation.
- Suppliers must not offer undisclosed kickbacks or off-invoice discounts to individual staff.
- Any change to unit price after quotation submission requires a revised written quotation.

Delivery and Quality Commitments:
- Delivered goods must match specifications on the purchase order; defective items are returned at supplier cost per standard supplier terms.
- Suppliers must disclose known stock limitations (partial fulfillment, MOQ) at quotation time.

Reporting Violations:
- SME staff or managers who observe code-of-conduct breaches must report to the procurement head within 5 working days.
- Repeated violations may result in removal from the approved supplier register (process not defined in this document).

Note: This addendum does not specify criminal penalties, court jurisdiction, or monetary fine schedules; those are governed by applicable law and separate legal contracts.
