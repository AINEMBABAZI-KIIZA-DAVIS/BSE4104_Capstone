Procurement Policy for Small Businesses in Uganda
Objective: To ensure value for money, transparency, and fairness in all procurement activities.

Thresholds:
- Micro-purchases (Under UGX 500,000): Direct procurement allowed.
- Small purchases (UGX 500,000 - 5,000,000): Minimum of three quotes required.
- Large purchases (Over UGX 5,000,000): Open tendering required.

Local Preference: Preference is given to locally registered suppliers within Uganda.
