Supplier Code of Conduct

- Compliance with Laws: Suppliers must comply with all applicable Ugandan laws and regulations.
- Anti-Corruption: No bribes, kickbacks, or other illegal payments may be offered or accepted.
- Labor Rights: Suppliers must not use child labor or forced labor. Fair wages must be paid.
- Environment: Suppliers are encouraged to adopt environmentally friendly practices.
- Conflict of Interest: Any potential conflict of interest must be disclosed immediately.
