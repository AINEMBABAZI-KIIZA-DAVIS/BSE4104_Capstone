Standard Supplier Terms and Conditions

2. Currency: All payments will be made in Uganda Shillings (UGX).

4. Quality: Goods must match the specifications in the Purchase Order. Defective goods will be returned at the supplier's cost.
5. Termination: Either party may terminate the agreement with 14 days written notice.
