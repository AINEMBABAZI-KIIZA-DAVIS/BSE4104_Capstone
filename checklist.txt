PROCUREMENT CHECKLIST – Goods and Services Tender
==================================================

Bidders must include ALL of the following documents in their submission.
Any item that is absent or cannot be clearly identified will be flagged as missing.

1. Company Registration Certificate
   - Valid certificate of incorporation or business registration issued by the
     relevant authority.

2. Tax Clearance Certificate
   - Current tax clearance certificate issued by the national revenue authority,
     valid at the time of submission.

3. Bid Security (Tender Bond)
   - Bank guarantee or surety bond for the required bid security amount,
     issued by an approved financial institution.

4. Technical Proposal
   - Detailed description of the bidder's approach, methodology, and work plan
     for delivering the required goods/services.

5. Financial Proposal (Itemised Price Schedule)
   - Fully completed price schedule with unit costs, quantities, and total bid
     price, denominated in the specified currency.

6. Company Profile and Capability Statement
   - Overview of the company, including organisational structure, key personnel,
     facilities, and demonstrated experience in similar contracts.

7. Reference Letters (Minimum Two)
   - At least two reference letters from previous clients confirming satisfactory
     performance on comparable contracts.

8. Signed Cover Letter (Original Signature Required)
   - Official cover letter on company letterhead, signed by an authorised
     representative, addressed to the procuring entity.
